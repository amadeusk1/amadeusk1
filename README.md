# Amadeus Kaczmarek

Computer Science student at the University of Calgary. I build software for problems I keep hitting myself: a workout log that stays private, a group chat that never finds a time, a syllabus full of dates, a fuel light I do not trust.

Current focus: Android development with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

## Projects

**[PRyx](https://github.com/amadeusk1/PRyx)**
Strength tracker for Android. Log personal records and bodyweight, then watch them on a dashboard of progress graphs. Everything stays on the device. [On Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.amadeusk.liftlog).

**[Orbit](https://github.com/amadeusk1/MyOrbit)**
An address book drawn as a solar system. People you talk to stay in close orbit. People you go quiet on drift outward until you reach out again. Contacts, texts, and calls never leave the phone.

**[DownTo](https://github.com/amadeusk1/DownTo)**
A way out of the "who is free?" group chat. Share when you are busy, see the overlap, and turn "we should hang out" into a time and a place.

**[CourseFlow](https://github.com/amadeusk1/CourseFlow)**
Chrome extension for University of Calgary D2L. It reads deadlines from D2L and course outline PDFs, puts them on a calendar, and exports an ics file.

**[Digital Fuel Gauge](https://github.com/amadeusk1/Digital-Fuel-Gauge)**
Android app for fuel left and driving range, from the odometer, consumption, and tank size. It handles more than one car, a fill history, and GPS trips. [Site and APK](https://amadeusk1.github.io/Digital-Fuel-Gauge/).

**[Notes+](https://github.com/amadeusk1/NotesPlus-Desktop-Productivity)**
A Windows 10 and 11 notepad with tabs, live Markdown, and a task list, for people who wanted the Windows 11 editor on an older machine.
